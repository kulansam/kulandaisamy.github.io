About

I am a postdoctoral research fellow at Boston Children’s Hospital / Harvard Medical School. My work integrates vascular biology with computational genomics, focusing on endothelial dysfunction and functional interpretation of transcriptomic data.

I develop SCIG / SCIGFun, an AI-driven framework to identify cell-identity genes (CIGs) and improve pathway enrichment across tissues and disease contexts.
