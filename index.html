About

I am a postdoctoral research fellow at Boston Children’s Hospital / Harvard Medical School. My research integrates vascular biology with computational genomics, focusing on endothelial dysfunction and functional interpretation of transcriptomic data.

I develop SCIG / SCIGFun, an AI-driven framework for identifying cell-identity genes and improving pathway enrichment across tissues and diseases.

Research Interests

  • Endothelial biology, angiogenesis/lymphangiogenesis, atherosclerosis
  • Single-cell and multi-omics analysis (scRNA-seq, RNA velocity)
  • AI-driven gene-set methods and pathway enrichment
  • Computational tools for translational biology
