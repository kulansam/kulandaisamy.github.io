About Me

I am a postdoctoral research fellow at Boston Children’s Hospital / Harvard Medical School. My research integrates vascular biology with computational genomics, focusing on endothelial dysfunction and functional interpretation of transcriptomic data.

I develop SCIG / SCIGFun, an AI-driven framework to identify cell-identity genes (CIGs) and improve pathway enrichment across tissues and disease contexts.

Contribution to Science

  • AI-driven gene-set methods: SCIG identifies cell-identity gene sets to improve functional enrichment.
  • Single-cell analysis pipelines: robust workflows for cell-type profiling, trajectories, and pathway interpretation.
  • Web resources: SCIGFun to make CIG-based enrichment accessible to the community.
