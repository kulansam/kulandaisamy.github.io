## About

I am **Dr. Kulandaisamy Arulsamy**, a postdoctoral research fellow at **Boston Children’s Hospital / Harvard Medical School**. My research integrates **vascular biology** with **computational genomics**, with a focus on endothelial dysfunction and functional interpretation of transcriptomic data.

I am developing **SCIG/SCIGFun**, an AI-driven framework to identify **cell-identity genes (CIGs)** and improve pathway enrichment across tissues and disease contexts.

---

## Research Interests
- Endothelial biology, angiogenesis/lymphangiogenesis, and atherosclerosis  
- Single-cell and multi-omics analysis (scRNA-seq, trajectories, RNA velocity)  
- AI-driven gene-set methods and pathway enrichment  
- Translational computational tools for biomedical research
